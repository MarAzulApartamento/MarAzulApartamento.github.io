# Design context — Apartamento Mar Azul

This document grounds every design skill invocation. Read it before generating any UI.

---

## Users

**Primary A — Couples (25–50)**
Travelling as two for a calm coastal break. Browse from a phone in bed at night, on a laptop at the kitchen table on a Sunday morning. Anxious about the gamble of an unknown short-stay rental. Want to know quickly: is this place real, is it nice, is it well-located, can I trust the host. Sensitive to hospitality clichés ("luxurious getaway," "dream escape") which trigger scam-radar.

**Primary B — Small families (2–4 guests, at least one young child)**
Practical, time-stressed. Need very fast answers to specific questions: crib? high chair? parking? Wi-Fi? They book on lunch breaks or after kids are asleep. Patience for marketing fluff is near zero.

Both audiences are international (UK, Germany, Netherlands, France, Portugal). English-default site with PT/DE/NL/FR translations. Booking-decision context: comparing this property side-by-side with 3-5 OTA listings. The site has 30 seconds to differentiate from a Booking.com listing.

---

## Brand personality

**Three words:** calm, coastal, quietly premium.

**Voice:** specific, calm, place-driven. Friend-of-a-friend recommendation tone, not concierge or hotel-marketing. Confident enough to skip exclamation marks.

**Anti-personality (avoid):**
- Wedding-invitation luxury
- Surfer / casual-beach-house
- Boutique-hotel-marketing-speak
- Tech-startup minimalism (overused fonts, gradient hero, etc.)
- Generic AI-coastal-rental aesthetic (Playfair Display headline + photo grid + "Book your dream getaway")

**Emotional goal on landing:** "this is a real place owned by a real person who takes care of it."

---

## Aesthetic direction

**Tone:** quiet editorial. Closer to a thoughtful Sintra cookbook or an independent Algarve travel zine than a hotel website. The interface should feel like it was set by a designer who cares, printed on warm uncoated paper.

**References (for mood, not visual copy):**
- Editorial magazines: Apartamento, Cabana, Cereal
- Boutique short-stay rentals that escape OTA-look: Plum Guide property pages (positioning, not visual), the.boon.club (typography ambition)
- Portuguese visual culture: hand-painted azulejo lettering, faded shop awnings, vintage Algarve travel posters

**Anti-references:**
- Generic Airbnb-clone holiday sites (Squarespace template look)
- Luxury-hotel sites (Mandarin Oriental, Aman) — too cold and corporate
- Anything with "ocean view," "luxurious," "dream," "escape," "ultimate" in headlines

**Theme:** light mode only. Browsing context is mid-day on a phone, kitchen-table evening on a laptop, Sunday afternoon planning. Dark mode is wrong for this audience and use case.

**Rules:**
- Background mostly Warm White; let photography dominate.
- Atlantic Blue reserved for CTAs, links, headings on tinted backgrounds, and the logo.
- Sun Sand only as small highlights, dividers, icon accents — sparingly.
- Error Red ONLY for inline form validation. Never decorative.
- Maximum 2 non-neutral hues per viewport.
- Tint neutrals subtly toward Atlantic Blue (chroma 0.005–0.01) for subconscious cohesion.
- Reduce chroma at extreme lightness so light-tint colors don't go garish.

### Typography (final)
- **Display:** Spectral (Production Type, SIL OFL via Google Fonts) — weights 400, 700 + 400 italic. Only at 24px and above.
- **Body / UI:** Manrope (SIL OFL via Google Fonts) — weights 400, 500, 600. Min 16px for body, max 68 characters per line.
- Type scale: 5-step, minimum 1.25× ratio between steps for clear hierarchy
- Sentence case in headings; never ALL CAPS for headings (only short labels)
- These replace both Playfair Display + Inter (`reflex_fonts_to_reject` AI defaults) and the brief PP Editorial New + Switzer detour (incompatible with Pangram Pangram's Free-for-Personal-Use EULA for our commercial site).

### Spacing scale (4pt base, semantic names)
- `--space-1`: 4 / `--space-2`: 8 / `--space-3`: 12 / `--space-4`: 16
- `--space-6`: 24 / `--space-8`: 32 / `--space-12`: 48 / `--space-16`: 64 / `--space-20`: 80
- Use `gap` (not margin) for sibling spacing. Vary spacing for hierarchy.

### Radius
- `--radius-sm: 4px` / `--radius-md: 8px` / `--radius-lg: 14px` / `--radius-full: 9999px`
- No rounded-rectangle-with-generic-shadow card pattern.

### Shadow (subtle, never decorative)
- `sm: 0 1px 3px rgba(29,38,48,0.08)`
- `md: 0 4px 14px rgba(29,38,48,0.10)`
- `lg: 0 12px 32px rgba(29,38,48,0.13)`

## Design principles (apply to every screen)

1. **Specificity over polish.** A specific local detail ("the pool is a 5-minute walk via the Rua das Pedras Negras path") beats a generic luxury phrase. Every paragraph names a real place, time, or constraint.

2. **Photography leads.** Layout exists to frame photos, not the other way round. Generous breathing room around hero shots. The terrace shot should feel like it could appear in a print magazine.

3. **Trust signals over decoration.** Real reviews from real names, response-time promises, host name and face, address with an embedded map. Decorative elements that don't serve trust get cut.

4. **Direct booking is the only conversion.** Every page has a path to `/book`. The Lodgify embed is the destination, but the journey through availability + pricing + amenities feels native to our brand.

5. **Restraint in motion.** One purposeful scroll-reveal moment on the homepage (golden-hour light pan over the terrace, Ken Burns micro-pan on hero photo). No bouncing, no parallax theatre, no scroll-jacking. Reduced-motion always honored.

6. **Anti-AI-slop self-test.** If the screenshot would make a designer say "AI made this" within 5 seconds — start over. Specifically avoid: gradient text, glassmorphism cards, left-border colored stripes, generic rounded-shadowed cards, centered-everything layouts, repeated icon-heading-text card grids.

## Constraints

### Technical
- Stack: Astro v6 (static output) + Tailwind v4 + Sveltia CMS
- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages, free tier
- No backend; Lodgify provides booking infrastructure via embedded widget on `/book`
- Lodgify API used at build time + client-side fetch for availability and rates display
- Free tier of all third-party services (Cloudflare Web Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Klaro cookie banner, Leaflet maps)

### Multilingual
- 7 languages: English (default at `/`), Portuguese (`/pt/`), Spanish (`/es/`), Italian (`/it/`), German (`/de/`), Dutch (`/nl/`), French (`/fr/`)
- All content edited via Sveltia CMS; per-language content in Markdown collections
- hreflang tags + per-language sitemaps generated automatically by Astro

### Voice rules
- **No em dashes anywhere on public-facing copy.** Use full stops or rewrite.
- Never reference "sea view," "ocean view," or "beachfront" as headline features (only "sea glimpse" lightly).
- Location framing: "walking distance from the centre of Lagos and the beaches." Avoid stating exact minutes-by-car or minutes-on-foot in marketing copy.
- Not pet-friendly — remove pets from all messaging.
- Direct booking incentives = perks (welcome bottle of vinho verde, late checkout) not price discounts (Booking.com rate parity).
- WhatsApp CTA always includes "we may answer within 12 hours."
- Audience-aware copy per piece: speak to couples OR families specifically, not both at once.
- All copy in British English (default); other languages localised per per-language SEO keyword research, not literal translation.

### Accessibility
- WCAG AA target across all pages
- Body text minimum 16px, line-length max 68 characters
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for the hero video and any scroll animations
- Form fields with explicit labels (no placeholder-only labels)
- Colour contrast tested against Warm White background

### Performance
- Astro `<Image>` for all photos (responsive srcset, AVIF + WebP)
- Hero video: MP4 + WebM, ≤10 MB total, autoplay muted with poster fallback
- Lazy-load images below the fold
- Preload only the 4 critical font WOFF2 files
- Target: Lighthouse Performance ≥ 95 mobile

### Business
- Single property: 2–4 guests, family-friendly, 1 bedroom + 1 sofa-bed equivalent
- Address: Rua Dom Luís da Silveira lote V 44 B, 8600-575 Lagos
- 27 historical bookings: Booking.com 16, Airbnb 10, Vrbo 1, **Direct 0** — the +40% direct goal is growth from zero
- Brand-name collision risk in Lagos (multiple "Mar Azul" properties); brand differentiation via specific address + unique photography is critical
- Lodgify is in production; all dashboard/API mutations require explicit owner confirmation

## Absolute bans (skills must enforce)

- `border-left:` / `border-right:` greater than 1px as a colored accent stripe (cards, list items, callouts, alerts)
- Gradient text (`background-clip: text` with any gradient)
- Glassmorphism cards (decorative blur + glow)
- Generic rounded-rectangle cards with drop shadows used as the primary content pattern
- Centered everything (use asymmetric, left-aligned typography for editorial feel)
- Repeated identical card grids (icon + heading + text, repeated endlessly)
- Playfair Display, Inter, Cormorant, Fraunces, Newsreader, Lora, Crimson, Syne, Space Grotesk, IBM Plex, DM Sans, DM Serif, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Sans/Serif (the AI-reflex font list — these are the BANNED ones, NOT our actual fonts)
- Em dashes on public-facing copy
- The phrases: "ocean view," "sea view," "beachfront," "luxurious," "ultimate," "dream," "escape," "unlock"
