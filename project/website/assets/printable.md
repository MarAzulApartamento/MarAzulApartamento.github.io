## \#\# PROMPT 1 — Business Card (Claude Design)

\> Paste this prompt into \*\*Claude Design\*\* to create the business card.

\---

Design a professional double-sided business card for Apartamento Mar Azul, a single independent short-stay apartment in Lagos, Algarve, Portugal.

BRAND SYSTEM (apply exactly):

* Primary colour: Atlantic Blue \#1E5B73  
* Secondary: Seafoam \#CFE3DE  
* Accent: Sun Sand \#D8B98A  
* Background: Warm White \#F8F6F1  
* Text: Deep Navy \#1D2630  
* Muted: Slate Grey \#6F7A86  
* Display font: Playfair Display (700) — for property name and hero text  
* Body font: Inter (400, 500\) — for all contact and detail information  
* Card size: 85mm × 55mm (standard EU business card)  
* Print safe zone: 3mm bleed on all edges, 5mm margin inside

FRONT SIDE:

* Background: Atlantic Blue \#1E5B73 full bleed  
* Logo: white reversed version — top-left, minimum 100px wide  
  (Icon: minimal single-line wave; Wordmark: "Apartamento" Inter 500 \+ "Mar Azul" Playfair Display 700, all white)  
* Centre headline (Playfair Display 700, white, 22pt):  
  "The best price is always here."  
* Sub-line (Inter 400, Seafoam \#CFE3DE, 10pt):  
  "Book direct and save. Guaranteed best rate on every platform."  
* Bottom strip: Warm White \#F8F6F1 thin horizontal band (8mm tall)  
  \- inside it, in Atlantic Blue Inter 600 small text: www.apartamentomarazul.com

BACK SIDE:

* Background: Warm White \#F8F6F1  
* Top section: property name "Apartamento Mar Azul" in Playfair Display 700 Atlantic Blue, left-aligned, 18pt  
* Sub-line below: "Lagos · Algarve · Portugal" in Inter 400 Slate Grey 9pt  
* Thin Sun Sand \#D8B98A horizontal rule (0.5pt) below the sub-line  
* Contact block (Inter 400, Deep Navy \#1D2630, 9pt, left-aligned, line-spaced at 1.6):  
  🌐 www.apartamentomarazul.com  
  📍 Lagos, Algarve, Portugal  
  🏖️ 5 min by car to Dona Ana Beach  
* Bottom-right: small QR code placeholder box (18mm × 18mm, rounded 4px corners, Seafoam \#CFE3DE fill, label in Inter 400 Slate Grey 7pt: "Scan to book direct")  
* Bottom-left: "Best rate guaranteed." in Playfair Display italic 400, Atlantic Blue, 10pt

DESIGN RULES:

* No gradients on any element.  
* No drop shadows.  
* No coloured circles behind icons.  
* No em dashes in any text — use a full stop or rewrite the line if needed.  
* Keep the front bold and minimal — the headline must be the visual anchor.  
* Keep the back clean and scannable — contact info must be legible at arm's length.  
* Deliver as a high-resolution flat mockup at 300 DPI, shown as front \+ back side-by-side on a neutral background.  
* Also deliver as individual front and back files ready for pr

## \#\# PROMPT 2 — Google Reviews QR Code Card / Table Tent (Claude Design)

\> Paste this prompt into \*\*Claude Design\*\* to create the physical review prompt piece.

\---

Design a physical printed piece for Apartamento Mar Azul that encourages guests to scan a QR code and leave a Google Review. This will be placed inside the apartment — on the kitchen counter or coffee table — so guests see it during their stay.

BRAND SYSTEM (apply exactly):

* Primary colour: Atlantic Blue \#1E5B73  
* Secondary: Seafoam \#CFE3DE  
* Accent: Sun Sand \#D8B98A  
* Background: Warm White \#F8F6F1  
* Text: Deep Navy \#1D2630  
* Muted: Slate Grey \#6F7A86  
* Display font: Playfair Display (700, 400 italic)  
* Body font: Inter (400, 500, 600\)

DELIVERABLE — design BOTH of the following formats:

### FORMAT A — A6 FLAT CARD (105mm × 148mm, landscape orientation):

* Background: Warm White \#F8F6F1 full bleed, 3mm bleed, 5mm margin  
* Top-left: logo (horizontal, Atlantic Blue primary version, 90mm wide)  
* Centre-left (60% of card width):  
  * Headline (Playfair Display 700, Deep Navy, 22pt, max 2 lines):  
    "Did you enjoy your stay?"  
  * Body (Inter 400, Slate Grey \#6F7A86, 10pt, max 3 lines):  
    "Your review helps other travellers find us and means the world to us. It only takes 30 seconds."  
  * CTA line (Inter 600, Atlantic Blue, 10pt):  
    "Scan to leave a Google Review →"  
* Centre-right (40% of card width):  
  * Large QR code box (60mm × 60mm, centred vertically in its column)  
  * QR code visual: placeholder square with rounded corners (radius 6px), Deep Navy border 1pt, interior fill Deep Navy with white squares pattern (mock QR style)  
  * Below QR code: "Google Reviews" in Inter 500, Slate Grey, 8pt, centred  
  * Small Google logo placeholder (coloured dots "G" mark, 14pt) above the "Google Reviews" label  
* Bottom strip: thin 6mm tall Atlantic Blue band full width  
  — inside: "Apartamento Mar Azul · Lagos, Algarve · www.apartamentomarazul.com" in Inter 400 white 7.5pt, centred

### FORMAT B — TABLE TENT / FOLDED CARD (A5 folded \= two A5 panels, 148mm × 105mm each, portrait): This is a bi-fold card that stands on a table like a tent sign.

FRONT PANEL (visible face when standing):

* Background: Atlantic Blue \#1E5B73 full bleed  
* Centre: wave icon (white, minimal single-line, 30mm wide) centred near top third  
* Below icon: "Apartamento Mar Azul" in Playfair Display 700 white, 20pt, centred  
* Below name: thin white rule (0.5pt, 60mm wide, centred)  
* Main message (Playfair Display 400 italic, white, 16pt, centred, max 2 lines):  
  "Enjoyed your stay?"  
* Sub-message (Inter 400, Seafoam \#CFE3DE, 9pt, centred):  
  "We would love to hear from you."

BACK PANEL (facing guest at the table):

* Background: Warm White \#F8F6F1  
* Top: "Leave us a Google Review" in Playfair Display 700 Atlantic Blue, 18pt, centred  
* Sub (Inter 400, Slate Grey, 9pt, centred, max 2 lines):  
  "Scan the QR code below with your phone camera."  
* QR code box: 65mm × 65mm, centred, rounded 6px corners, Deep Navy border 1pt, mock QR pattern fill  
* Below QR: "Google Reviews" label with Google G icon placeholder, Inter 500, Slate Grey, 8pt, centred  
* Bottom: "Thank you. It means the world to us 🌊" in Playfair Display 400 italic, Atlantic Blue, 10pt, centred  
* Footer: thin Sun Sand \#D8B98A rule (0.5pt) then "www.apartamentomarazul.com" in Inter 400 Slate Grey 7pt, centred

DESIGN RULES for both formats:

* No gradients.  
* No drop shadows.  
* No generic stock imagery.  
* No em dashes in any text — use a full stop or rewrite the line if needed.  
* QR code box must be clearly the visual focal point on the review-action panel.  
* All text must be legible when printed at actual size — body text minimum 8pt.  
* Deliver flat print-ready mockups at 300 DPI.  
* Show FORMAT A as a flat card mockup.  
* Show FORMAT B as an assembled standing table tent photo-realistic mockup (3D angled view).

NOTE ON QR CODE: Replace the placeholder QR box with the actual Google Maps review link QR code for the property once the Google Business Profile URL is confirmed. The link format will be:  
https://g.page/r/\[PLACE\_ID\]/review
