**Goal:** sit on the dining table for the entire stay. Answer most "what / how / who" questions a guest has in their first hour, then keep working as a quiet reminder for the rest of the stay — house rules, emergency contacts, SUP rental, and a calm Google-review nudge near the end.

This is the **centrepiece** of the in-apartment print set. Everything else (fridge magnet, apartment guide folder) revolves around it.

---

## What this is

A vertical desk flip stand — same product family as `https://www.joom.com/pt/products/69c501704ea9ac017df807d7`. Eight content panels per side, two sides (one Portuguese, one English). One stand per apartment. The guest turns it around 180° to switch language. The flip pages let the stand hold more content than a static tent without overwhelming any one page.
