# Welcome card — print spec

**Last updated:** 2026-05-08
**Status:** drafted, awaiting Stefania's review + content (item #8 in `questions-for-stefania.md`).
**Goal:** sit somewhere visible inside the apartment (the dining table or kitchen counter) and answer 80% of the questions a guest will have in their first 30 minutes — Wi-Fi, trash, who-to-call, where-to-eat, what-to-do — without them needing to message the host.

---

## Purpose

Two related jobs:

1. **Practical onboarding** — Wi-Fi credentials, key location, check-out instructions, emergency contacts. Without this, every stay starts with a "what's the Wi-Fi?" WhatsApp.
2. **Brand-voiced local recommendation** — "the host's favourite tasca, padaria, quiet beach" line on the homepage promised this. The welcome card delivers it.

Done well, this is the single most-loved object in the apartment. It's also a quiet brand object — readers feel hosted by a real person.

---

## Format

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Trim size unfolded | **A4 — 210 × 297 mm** |
| Fold | **Tri-fold** (folded into thirds, A4 → 99 × 210 mm finished) |
| Sides | **Two-sided** (six panels visible total: three on each side when fully unfolded) |
| Bleed | 3 mm each side |
| Paper | **300 gsm uncoated** (matte/natural, not coated). Uncoated reads as warmer and more "letter-from-the-host" than coated. |
| Finish | No lamination |
| Colour | CMYK |

A tri-fold A4 is light enough to feel personal, big enough to hold real content, and folds away cleanly when not being read.

## Per-locale variants

The welcome card matters more than the other print pieces for non-English speakers — they're trying to operate the apartment, not just leave a review. **Recommendation: print all 4 brand languages** (EN / PT / ES / IT — the languages Stefania actually speaks). Skip DE/NL/FR — guests of those nationalities almost always read English well enough at this level.

Stack four variants in a small basket on the dining table. Or print one card with EN on the front three panels and PT on the back three (rejected: too crowded, fights the layout).

Going with stacked variants: 4 versions, one per language Stefania speaks.

## Production

| Vendor | Cost (20 tri-folds) | Lead time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOO | doesn't stock A4 tri-folds | – | – |
| Vistaprint (Brochure → Tri-fold A4 product) | ~€35 for 20 | 5-7 days | Standard product, good results. |
| Local Lagos shop | ~€40-60 | 2-3 days | Better paper choice (uncoated stock); slight upcharge worth it for the warmer feel. **Recommended.** |

**Quantity:** 20 cards (5 per language × 4 languages). Replace when down to 3 of any language. Cards stay in the apartment so they last a long time — only printed when content changes.

**Proof check:**

- [ ] Tri-fold panels are equal width (33% each)
- [ ] Score lines are pre-creased so the card folds cleanly without tearing
- [ ] Wi-Fi password is legible — sometimes special characters get garbled in low-DPI printing. Print at 300 DPI minimum.
- [ ] Cover (Panel 1) Atlantic Blue matches the business card
- [ ] All four language variants have the same layout (only the content varies)
