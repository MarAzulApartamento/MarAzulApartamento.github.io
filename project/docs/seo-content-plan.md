# SEO content plan

**Last updated:** 2026-05-08
**Status:** drafted, awaiting Stefania's review (item #26 in `questions-for-stefania.md`).
**Sprint:** 4 (SEO content, 10–15 articles per language).
**Goal:** publish a cluster of long-form pages that earn organic search traffic from couples and small families researching a Lagos / Algarve trip, and route them toward a direct booking on `apartamentomarazul.com/book`.

---

## Strategy in one paragraph

We don't need *more* generic Algarve content. The web has plenty. We need content that's specific to the way **Apartamento Mar Azul guests** actually use Lagos — walking distances, quiet beaches, tascas vs tourist restaurants, shoulder-season honesty, what works for couples vs families. Each article answers a question a real searcher types, weaves the apartment into the answer naturally (never as a hard sell), and ends with one clear path to `/book`. Total cluster size: **12 articles per language × 7 languages = 84 pages**, written from one English master and localised per market in the translation pass after Stefania's content review.

Each article serves three purposes:

1. **Rank** for a long-tail query that has search volume but isn't already saturated by Booking.com / TripAdvisor / Wanderlog aggregator pages.
2. **Convert** — the reader who landed on a "what to do in Lagos" page should leave with the apartment as the obvious place to stay.
3. **Internal linking** — tighten the site's internal link graph so the homepage and `/book` page accumulate authority from this content.

---

## The 12 article cluster

Each entry below has: **title** (H1 / meta title), **slug**, **target search query** (what people would type), **audience**, **why it ranks** (search-supply argument), **chapters** (H2 outline), **internal links**, **rough word count**.

### 1. Lagos in shoulder season: when and why to visit in May, June and October

- **Slug:** `/journal/lagos-shoulder-season`
- **Target query:** *"Lagos Portugal in October"*, *"Algarve in May"*, *"shoulder season Algarve"*
- **Audience:** couples planning a less-touristy trip; price-conscious families avoiding August
- **Why it ranks:** sustained year-round search volume; current results are mostly thin aggregator pages, room for a more specific, host-voiced answer
- **Chapters:**
  1. May, June, October — what each month is actually like
  2. Water temperature, sunshine hours, crowds (a small comparison table)
  3. What's open and what's closed
  4. Why direct booking is cheapest in shoulder season (no surge from OTAs)
  5. Walking the coast path: best sections for shoulder season
- **Internal links:** `/book`, the homepage Location section
- **Word count:** ~1100

## Per-language strategy

Twelve master articles in **English** (Sprint 4 first); translate to **PT, ES, IT, DE, NL, FR** in a localisation pass after Stefania approves. Localisation isn't pure translation — keyword research per market substitutes 1-2 articles where the local query landscape differs:

| Locale | Articles to substitute | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 🇵🇹 PT | Drop #4 (coast path) — replace with *"Lagos para portugueses: como aproveitar uma cidade que finge ser para turistas"*. Drop #11 (packing) — replace with *"Os melhores fins-de-semana fora da época em Lagos"*. | Domestic readers don't search for English-tourist content. |
| 🇪🇸 ES | Same 12 (Spanish travellers' search behaviour mirrors EN). Localise place names where applicable. |
| 🇮🇹 IT | Stefania speaks IT natively; consider an extra article in her voice for #12 expanded. |
| 🇩🇪 DE | Substitute #11 with *"Algarve im Winter: warum die Deutschen ihn unterschätzen"* — sustained volume in DACH market. |
| 🇳🇱 NL | Add a #13 *"Lagos in oktober: wat je echt kunt verwachten"* — strong NL November-trip market. |
| 🇫🇷 FR | Drop #4, replace with *"Lagos pour un week-end prolongé en mai"* — French long-weekender peak. |

Total page count: 12 × 7 = 84, with ~6-8 substitutions = **roughly 90 pages of original or near-original content**.

## Production cadence

We can't publish 84 articles in one sprint. Realistic schedule:

- **Sprint 4:** 5 articles in EN (the high-traffic ones — #1, #5, #6, #9, #10).
- **Sprint 4.5:** 5 more in EN (#2, #3, #4, #7, #11).
- **Sprint 4.5 continued:** EN articles #8, #12.
- **Sprint 5+:** Localisation pass — translate + substitute + publish. Two languages per sprint (PT and IT first; DE and ES next; NL and FR last).

Each EN article: ~4-6 hours of writing + light SEO check (heading hierarchy, meta description, internal links). Total ~50-70 hours of writing across the 12 articles.

## Article structural template

Every article follows the same skeleton so the site reads as one voice:

```
Article URL: /journal/<slug>
H1: <Title>
Lede paragraph (2-3 sentences setting the question)
H2: <chapter 1>
   Body, ~150-200 words
H2: <chapter 2>
... etc, 4-6 chapters total
H2: <closing chapter — almost always action-oriented>
   Soft CTA — "want the apartment? check availability"
   Link to /book
```

Each article also gets:

- A **meta title** (≤60 chars) and **meta description** (≤160 chars) keyword-optimised but brand-voiced.
- One **lead photo** at the top (existing photos from the gallery, no stock).
- 2-3 **internal links** to other journal articles or the homepage / `/book`.
- **JSON-LD `Article` schema** in the HTML head (Astro can generate this from the markdown frontmatter).
