These are the terms that apply when you book a stay at **Apartamento Mar Azul** in Lagos, Algarve. They form the contract between you and the host. Please read them before booking. By completing a booking you confirm you've read and accepted them.

## The property

- **Address:** Rua Dom Luís da Silveira lote V 44 B, 8600-575 Lagos, Portugal.
- **Capacity:** 2 to 4 guests.
- **Alojamento Local registration:** [TO CONFIRM — AL number]. Apartamento Mar Azul is registered as a short-stay rental with the Portuguese tourism authority.

## How to book

You can book through:

- **This website** — the calendar on the homepage and the `/book` page show real-time availability and prices.
- **Booking.com, Airbnb, Vrbo** — same calendar, same property.

Booking direct on this site usually gives you the best available rate and a few perks (free parking, late check-out and early check-in when the calendar allows, a direct WhatsApp line with the host).

## Payment

Payment is taken in full at the time of booking. We accept the cards supported by Stripe through our booking partner Lodgify. Prices are shown in euros.

## Cancellation

- More than **7 days before arrival**: full refund.
- Within **7 days of arrival**: 50% of the booking is non-refundable; the other 50% is refunded.
- No-show or early departure: no refund for nights not stayed.

To cancel a direct booking, write to the contact email or WhatsApp the host. For bookings made via Booking.com, Airbnb or Vrbo, cancel through that platform.

## Check-in and check-out

- **Check-in:** from 15:00. Earlier arrival is sometimes possible — message us in advance.
- **Check-out:** by 11:00. Late check-out is sometimes possible — message us the day before.
- Self check-in details (key location, code) are sent to you 48 hours before arrival.

## House rules

- **No smoking** anywhere inside the apartment or on the terrace.
- **No pets.** The property is not pet-friendly.
- **No parties or events.** The maximum number of guests in the apartment at any time is the number you booked for, capped at 4.
- **Quiet hours** between 22:00 and 08:00, in line with local rules in Lagos.
- Please leave the apartment in a reasonable state at check-out: dishes washed or in the dishwasher, rubbish in the bin, windows closed.

Breaking these rules may end the stay early without a refund.

## Damage

The apartment is checked between stays. If something is broken or missing, you agree to cover the reasonable repair or replacement cost. We'll always tell you what we found and share the receipt before charging anything.

## Liability

The host is responsible for keeping the apartment safe and in good condition. The host is not responsible for events outside their control: weather, public works, neighbour noise, internet outages, public transport disruption, or your personal items left behind. Travel insurance is your responsibility and is recommended for trips you've prepaid.

## Force majeure

If circumstances outside both parties' control (natural disaster, government order, fire, flood) prevent the stay, we'll either reschedule the booking or refund it. This applies in both directions.

## Personal data

We process your personal data to fulfil the booking and meet our tax obligations in Portugal. See the [Privacy Notice](/privacy/) for the full picture.

## Governing law

These terms are governed by Portuguese law. Any dispute that cannot be resolved between us will be heard by the courts of Lagos, Portugal.
